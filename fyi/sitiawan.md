# Historical Sites in Sitiawan (Early 20th Century & Colonial Period)

List of historical places and sites in Sitiawan, Perak, Malaysia associated with the British colonial administration, the 1903 Foochow Christian settlement led by the American Methodist Mission, and early rubber planting in the Dindings hinterland, ordered chronologically by built or establishment period.

## Chronological List

1. **Kampong Sitiawan River Jetty & Old Settlement (Pengkalan Kampung Sitiawan / "Mena Jetty" Site / Jeti Nelayan Kampung Sitiawan)** (1892)
   - Constructed in early 1892 at the Sitiawan port settlement on Sungai Sitiawan (tributary entering the Dindings estuary). Known historically as "Mena Jetty" (Pengkalan Kampung Sitiawan), it served as an important commercial shipping and local administrative center connecting the Dindings territory with Penang and Singapore. After Lumut and Sitiawan retroceded from the Straits Settlements back to Perak in 1934–1935 (losing free-trade port status), heavy river siltation and declining maritime traffic from Penang caused the jetty to gradually lose its shipping function. Located at Kampung Sitiawan / modern Jeti Nelayan Kampung Sitiawan off Lebuhraya Ipoh–Lumut.
   - Location: [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/547107075) (4.2426°N, 100.6944°E) | [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=Jeti+Nelayan+Kampung+Sitiawan,+32000+Sitiawan,+Perak)

2. **Pioneer Foochow Settlement Wells & Landing Site (Kampung Koh Pioneer Well)** (September 1903)
   - Historic hand-dug communal well sunk by the pioneer group of 363 Foochow (Kutien/Minqing) Christian immigrants who arrived under the sponsorship of the British colonial government and Methodist missionaries (Reverend William G. Shellabear and Dr. H. L. E. Luering). Located in the Pioneer Methodist Church / Memorial Hall complex in Kampung Koh.
   - Location: [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/840409214) (4.1963°N, 100.6999°E) | [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=Pioneer+Memorial+Hall,+Jalan+Lin+Chen+Mei,+Kampung+Koh,+32000+Sitiawan,+Perak)

3. **Pioneer Methodist Church (Gereja Methodist Pioneer Kampung Koh)** (1903; present sanctuary built 1927)
   - Founded immediately upon the arrival of the Foochow colonists in 1903 as the spiritual, educational, and social centre of the agrarian community; the brick structure completed in 1927 remains one of the area's premier heritage landmarks.
   - Location: [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/840409215) (4.1966°N, 100.6998°E) | [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=Pioneer+Methodist+Church,+Jalan+Lin+Chen+Mei,+Kampung+Koh,+32000+Sitiawan,+Perak)

4. **Lok Hop Dispensary / Dato' Dr. Lim Sim Heng Clinic Building (六合大药房 / 林心汉药房)** (1931–1932; clinic opened 1934)
   - Historic corner commercial landmark on Kampung Koh's main road junction leading toward Kampung Cina (福清洋). Constructed in 1931–1932, it was the first reinforced concrete/cement structure built in Kampung Koh, bearing the raised pediment relief "LOK HOP DISPENSARY" and Chinese characters "六合大药房". Originally opened as a dispensary in 1934 by Dr. Huang (黄医生), it was taken over in 1947 by prominent community doctor and philanthropist Dato' Dr. Lim Sim Heng (拿督林心汉医生), who served generations of rubber tappers and villagers, and later continued by his son Dr. Lim Hee Hong (林希芳医生) while preserving vintage 1930s medical cabinetry and registers.
   - Location: [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/?mlat=4.1965&mlon=100.6983#map=19/4.1965/100.6983) (4.1965°N, 100.6983°E) | [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=Lok+Hop+Dispensary,+Kampung+Koh,+32000+Sitiawan,+Perak)

5. **Sitiawan Settlement Heritage Museum (Muzium Warisan Sitiawan)** (1935)
   - Originally constructed in 1935 as the two-storey timber and concrete residence for the pastors of Pioneer Methodist Church; converted in 2003 into a community heritage museum preserving artifacts, photographs, and records of the Foochow pioneer settlement. Located directly adjacent to Pioneer Methodist Church.
   - Location: [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/840409216) (4.1962°N, 100.7002°E) | [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=Sitiawan+Settlement+Museum,+K6,+Jln+Lin+Chen+Mei,+Kampung+Koh,+32000+Sitiawan,+Perak)

6. **SMJK Nan Hwa / Anglo-Chinese School (Nan Hwa / ACS Sitiawan Heritage Grounds)** (1930s)
   - Educational institutions founded through collaboration between Methodist missionaries and the Foochow community to provide modern vernacular and English education, forming the backbone of civic life in Sitiawan. Includes ACS Sitiawan on Jalan Lin Chen Mei and SMJK Nan Hwa along Federal Route 5.
   - Location: [OpenStreetMap (ACS Sitiawan)](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/546785381) (4.1967°N, 100.7015°E) | [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=Sekolah+Methodist+ACS+Sitiawan,+Jalan+Lin+Chen+Mei,+Kampung+Koh,+32000+Sitiawan,+Perak)

7. **Kampung Koh Main Commercial Street & Shophouses** (1920s–1930s)
   - The economic heart of the early Foochow settlement along Jalan Pasar / Federal Route 5, lined with pre-war shophouses famous for traditional cottage industries such as hand-made Foochow longevity noodles (*mee sua*) and Kampung Koh chili sauce.
   - Location: [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/?mlat=4.1960&mlon=100.6985#map=18/4.1960/100.6985) (4.1960°N, 100.6985°E) | [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=Jalan+Pasar,+Kampung+Koh,+32000+Sitiawan,+Perak)
