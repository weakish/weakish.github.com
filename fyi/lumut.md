# Historical Sites in Lumut (British Colonial Period)

List of historical places and sites in Lumut, Perak, Malaysia associated with the British Straits Settlements administration (1874–1935) when Lumut served as the administrative headquarters of the Dindings territory, ordered chronologically by built or establishment period.

## Chronological List

1. **Lumut Government Jetty & Harbour Front (Terminal Jeti Lumut)** (1880s–1930s)
   - The principal deep-water harbour and administrative landing point for the Dindings under the Straits Settlements. Connected Lumut by government steam launches to Pangkor Island, and by coastal steamers to Penang and Singapore.
   - Location: [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/546124668) (4.2365°N, 100.6324°E) | [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=Terminal+Jeti+Lumut,+Jalan+Sultan+Yussuf+Izuddin,+32200+Lumut,+Perak)

2. **Dindings District Office & Court House Site (Pejabat Orang Besar Jajahan Manjung / Old Government Office Site)** (Late 19th c., ca. 1888)
   - Headquarters of the British District Officer / Assistant Resident of the Dindings, who reported to the Resident Councillor of Penang. Administered land revenue, civil disputes, and local governance until the territory's retrocession to Perak in 1935. Currently the site of the Orang Besar Jajahan / district administration complex.
   - Location: [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/546287091) (4.2335°N, 100.6312°E) | [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=Pejabat+Orang+Besar+Jajahan+Manjung,+Jalan+Telah+Muroh,+32200+Lumut,+Perak)

3. **Lumut Government Rest House Site (Rumah Rehat MARA / Old Rest House Site)** (1890s)
   - Colonial bungalow rest house built on the waterfront headland overlooking the Dindings estuary for visiting British officials from Penang and Singapore, later known as the Rest House Lumut (now the site of Rumah Rehat MARA / Orient Star vicinity).
   - Location: [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1274612425) (4.2317°N, 100.6281°E) | [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=Rumah+Rehat+MARA,+Jalan+Sultan+Idris+Shah,+32200+Lumut,+Perak)

4. **Lumut Colonial Post & Telegraph Office (Pejabat Pos Lumut)** (ca. 1890s–1900s)
   - The primary communications hub of the Dindings, handling British Straits Settlements postal stamps, telegraph dispatches, and incoming/outgoing mailbags ferried to and from Pangkor Island. Located on Jalan Sultan Idris Shah.
   - Location: [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/546124673) (4.2356°N, 100.6323°E) | [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=Pejabat+Pos+Lumut,+Jalan+Sultan+Idris+Shah,+32200+Lumut,+Perak)

5. **Lumut District Hospital Site (Hospital Lumut / Government Quarters Hill)** (1890s–1920s)
   - Colonial government hospital and medical quarters established on the hillside overlooking the town to service the Dindings mainland and island communities, treat malaria and beriberi, and manage public health quarantine along the shipping route.
   - Location: [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/?mlat=4.2315&mlon=100.6300#map=18/4.2315/100.6300) (4.2315°N, 100.6300°E) | [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=Bukit+Kerajaan,+Jalan+Telah+Muroh,+32200+Lumut,+Perak)

6. **Lumut Main Town Street (Jalan Sultan Idris Shah / Jalan Esplanade)** (Turn of 20th c.)
   - The colonial settlement's commercial street running along the sheltered shoreline behind the waterfront, lined with early shophouses and administrative quarters.
   - Location: [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/546124683) (4.2325°N, 100.6286°E) | [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=Jalan+Sultan+Idris+Shah,+Pekan+Lumut,+32200+Lumut,+Perak)

7. **Bukit Engku Busu Trig Station & Forest Reserve** (Early 20th c.)
   - High granitic hill (~331 m) behind Lumut town, gazetted as a protected forest reserve and used by the Survey Department of the Straits Settlements and Federated Malay States as a primary trigonometric triangulation station.
   - Location: [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/7932870248) (4.2229°N, 100.6284°E) | [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps?q=Bukit+Engku+Busu,+32200+Lumut,+Perak)
